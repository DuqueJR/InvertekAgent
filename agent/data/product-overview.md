## Overview

Where the drive has been stored for some time prior to installation, or has remained without the main power supply present for an extended period of time, it is necessary to reform the DC capacitors within the drive according to the following table before operation. For drives which have not been connected to the main power supply for a period of more than 2 years, this requires a reduced mains voltage to be applied for a time period, and gradually increased prior to operating the drive. The voltage levels are relative to the drive rated voltage. Following completion of the procedure, the drive may be operated as normal.

## Reforming schedule (verbatim from the table, p.6)

| Storage Period / Power-OFF Period | T1 Input Voltage | T1 Time | T2 Input Voltage | T2 Time | T3 Input Voltage | T3 Time | T4 Input Voltage | T4 Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Up to 1 Year | 100% | N/A | – | – | – | – | – | – |
| 1 – 2 Years | 100% | 1 Hour | N/A | – | – | – | – | – |
| 2 – 3 Years | 25% | 30 Minutes | 50% | 30 Minutes | 75% | 30 Minutes | 100% | 30 Minutes |
| More than 3 Years | 25% | 2 Hours | 50% | 2 Hours | 75% | 2 Hours | 100% | 2 Hours |

Note: the source prints the double phrase "a reduced mains voltage mains voltage"; transcribed here once as "a reduced mains voltage".
