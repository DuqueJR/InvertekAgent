## General (Section 3.1, p.9)

- The Optidrive should be mounted in a vertical position only, on a flat, flame resistant, vibration free mounting using the integral mounting holes or DIN Rail clip (Frame Sizes 1 and 2 only).
- IP20 Optidrives are designed to be installed in suitable enclosures to protect them from the environment.
- Do not mount flammable material close to the Optidrive.
- Ensure that the ambient temperature range does not exceed the permissible limits (Section 9.1, p.36).
- Provide suitable clean, moisture and contaminant free cooling air sufficient to fulfil the cooling requirements of the Optidrive.

## Enclosure mounting clearances (Section 3.4, p.10)

For drives mounted in non-ventilated metallic enclosures, Invertek Drives recommend the following minimum clearances (X = Above & Below, Y = Either Side, Z = Between drives side-by-side):

| Drive Size | X mm | Y mm | Z mm | Recommended airflow CFM |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 50 | 50 | 33 | 11 |
| 2 | 75 | 50 | 46 | 22 |
| 3 | 100 | 50 | 52 | 60 |
| 4 | 100 | 50 | 52 | 120 |
| 5 | 200 | 25 | 70 | 104 |

Enclosures should be made from a thermally conductive material. Where ventilated enclosures are used, there should be venting above the drive and below the drive; air should be drawn in below the drive and expelled above the drive. High moisture, salt or chemical content environments should use a suitably sealed (non-vented) enclosure. Typical drive heat losses are 3% of operating load conditions. NOTE (verbatim): "Above are guidelines only and the operating ambient temperature of the drive MUST be maintained at all times."
